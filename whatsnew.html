By who uses it

Every feature, for each person.

S&C coaches, gyms and clubs

  • Who needs you today. A home list of the athletes who are flagged or worth watching.
  • Roster. Every athlete and their status on one screen.
  • Athlete overview. Their week, tests and readiness, with a Message button.
  • Studio. Build and edit programmes by block and week. Copy, paste, undo, and choose how far a change goes.
  • Training themes. Set each exercise’s theme (power, strength, hypertrophy, strength-endurance, endurance, or your own) across the weeks you choose.
  • Season timeline. Zoom from the season to a block to a week.
  • Exercise library with demo videos.
  • Testing. Search for an athlete and see their results. Enter a whole group’s results in one grid, with a check when a number looks like a typo. Hawkin force plate results arrive by themselves once connected.
  • Wellness. Every athlete’s morning check-in scores in one place.
  • Load. Each athlete’s 7-day load against their 28-day average, flagged when it climbs.
  • Messages to one athlete or the whole squad.
  • Calendar. Add, edit and cancel events for one athlete, several or a squad: training, games, testing, appointments and more, with repeats.
  • Alerts.
  • Rack iPad on the gym floor. Pair it with a QR code or a 6-digit code; athletes find their name and log their sets.
More for s&c coaches

Clinics and physios

  • Limits. Set, change and end an athlete’s limits. Studio won’t save a session that breaks them, and the athlete sees them on Today.
  • Physio sign-in. See the roster, each athlete’s check-ins and the calendar.
  • Private messages. Physio and athlete threads that coaches can’t read.
  • The same athlete as the coach. One record, so nobody works from an old copy.
More for clinics

Athletes

  • Today first. Today’s session opens first, with a shorter version when you’re short on time.
  • Session player. Log each set, with a rest timer, effort (RPE) for each set and the whole session, and a line when you beat your best.
  • Finish with a note. Rate the session and leave your coach a note.
  • Works with no signal. Your plan is saved on your phone and sets sync later.
  • Daily check-in. Six scores out of 10: sleep, fatigue, soreness, mood, stress and energy. If you’re sore, mark where on a body chart.
  • Your load and goals. See your training load, flagged if it spikes, and track your goals in Progress.
  • Your own sessions. Add sessions you do yourself to your calendar.
  • Journal. Private to you.
  • Cycle tracking. Off until you turn it on. Staff see a summary only if you choose to share it.
  • Messages with your coach and your physio, and coach notes you can mark “Got it”.
  • Your schedule for the week and month.
  • On iPhone and Android. Get AiMS from the App Store or Google Play, or use it in your phone’s browser.
  • Your account. Delete it from the app at any time.
