Signing in

  • On the sign-in screen, choose your organisation first, then enter the email your coach or clinic has on file.
  • Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? and we will email you a link.
  • No login yet? Ask your coach or clinic to invite you.

Notifications

AiMS asks for permission before sending notifications. You can turn them on or off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the menu (the round button, bottom right), choose Profile, then Delete my account. Your login and contact details are removed straight away. Your organisation keeps your training and clinical records as the law requires; see our privacy policy.

Your information

To see or correct what is recorded about you, ask your organisation or email us. Our privacy policy explains what we collect and where it is kept.
