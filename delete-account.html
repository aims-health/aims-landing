Delete your AiMS account

Last updated 20 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the AiMS app (AiMS Health, by Athlete Intelligence Management Systems Pty Ltd), what is deleted, and what is kept.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the AiMS app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account (below Sign out).
  4. Read what will be deleted and what is kept, then tap Delete my account again to confirm.

It takes effect straight away. You are signed out on every device and cannot sign in with that account again.

Can't use the app?

Email tim@aimshealth.com.au from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my AiMS account” and the name of your gym, club or clinic. We will confirm it is you, delete the account within 30 days and email you when it is done. You don't need to reinstall the app.

Staff accounts and parent or guardian accounts are deleted this way too, or by your organisation.

What is deleted

Deleted straight awayDetails
Your loginYour email address and mobile number used to sign in, your passkeys and authenticator codes, and every signed-in session
Contact detailsThe email and phone number on your account, and app notification registrations for your devices

What is kept, and for how long

You train with an organisation (a gym, club, team or clinic). The records of that training and care belong to them and are kept by them:

Kept by your organisationHow long
Your name, date of birth and sport; your programme, the sessions and sets you logged, testing results and wellness check-insWhile your organisation uses AiMS, or until it asks us to delete them
Clinical notes written by registered practitionersFor the period the law requires for health records, generally seven years from the last entry, or until you turn 25 if the records were made while you were under 18
Records we must keep to show what happened, such as consent records and security logsFor as long as the law requires

Backups of our database are kept for up to 31 days , then overwritten. To ask about records your organisation keeps, contact them; we will help them respond.
