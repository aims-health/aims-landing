Billing

How billing works.

  • Billed monthly by card.
  • Change plan at any time. It applies from your next month.
  • Cancel at the end of any month. No exit fees.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. 30 days with your own athletes, no card needed. It ends on its own.

Do athletes pay?

Not on an organisation plan.
